White Hat Stories Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

The White Hat Stories uploader ("the app") runs on our own computer and uploads White Hat Stories videos to a connected TikTok account.

What we collect

  • When you connect TikTok, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar) from TikTok.
  • When posting, the app reads the privacy options TikTok offers for the account so you can choose one.

We do not collect anything else, and we do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

Only to upload the videos you choose and to show which account is connected. The token and profile information are stored on our computer, are never sold or shared, and are not sent anywhere except back to TikTok.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep the token only while the app is connected. Disconnect the app in your TikTok settings, or ask us at the address below, and we delete everything the app stored about you.

Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 18.
